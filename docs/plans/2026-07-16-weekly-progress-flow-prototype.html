STEP 03

Route notifications by time semantics.

The notification layer is not a second task system. Nothing is created without a reliable time; with one, routing depends on whether the item occupies a time slot.

✓Deadline actions → Reminders.
✓Meetings, reviews, or explicit slots → Calendar.
✓Unclear times → Apple Notes only.

STEP 04

Review facts; roll over what still matters.

The weekend review reads this week's facts from Apple Notes and shows done, remaining, blocked, and next week's direction. Only still-valid open items roll over.

✓Past weeks keep their original state and are never rewritten with new-week results.
✓Rollover reuses or updates linked notifications to avoid duplicates.
✓Users can hand-edit outside the managed region at any time.
